A Little About Me

My journey in software engineering started with an intrinsic curiosity for how code can solve everyday frictions. Over the years, I've cultivated a deep appreciation for the entire spectrum of engineering — from thoughtful frontend layout systems to robust, well-tested backend microservices and databases.
